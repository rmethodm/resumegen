# Example Prompts

## Prompts That Should Trigger This Skill

- "Rewrite my resume summary for a senior product designer role."
- "Make this professional summary shorter and more credible."
- "Tailor my summary to a B2B SaaS marketing manager job."

## Prompts That Should Not Trigger This Skill

- "Rewrite my entire resume."
- "Write a cover letter for this application."
- "Turn my experience into bullet points."

## Before / After Direction

Weak:

`Experienced professional with a strong background in leadership and communication.`

Stronger:

`Product leader with 8 years of experience scaling B2B SaaS teams, launching retention-focused workflows, and improving activation through data-informed experimentation.`
