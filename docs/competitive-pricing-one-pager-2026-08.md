# Resumegen competitive & pricing one-pager (2026-08)

> Decision aid. Full write-up: `docs/competitive-research-resumegen-2026-08.md`.  
> App is free/unlimited today; no billing live. Re-verify competitor prices before launch.

---

## Product in one line

**Free end-to-end apply workflow:** build → export PDF/DOCX → share → self-score → Kanban track → job import → browser autofill. Not a one-shot resume generator.

---

## Peer map

| Closest peers | Why |
|---------------|-----|
| **Teal, Jobscan, Rezi** | Builder + track / browser / ATS-match OS |
| Kickresume, Reactive Resume | Strong builders only |
| Zety, Resume.io | Download-paywall builders |

---

## Scorecard

| Dimension | Resumegen | Market |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Free unlimited PDF + DOCX | **Lead / match** | Many gate PDF; Rezi free = 1 resume / 3 PDFs |
| Free core (no tier wall) | **Lead** | Download-gated SaaS is common |
| Kanban tracker | **Match** | Teal et al. |
| Form autofill extension | **Match Rezi-class** | Closest peer: Rezi |
| ATS / resume-vs-JD score | **Lag** | Jobscan, Rezi, Kickresume |
| One-click board clip | **Lag** | Teal, Jobscan |
| Broad AI (letters, Auto Apply) | **Lag** | Peers ship more; ours optional & narrow |

**Keep free:** export, templates, resumes, share, Kanban, extension fill, job import.  
**Do not sell as premium alone:** PDF/DOCX (parity).

---

## What to charge (today)

| Choice | Price | Use when |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Stay free | $0 | Growth / no paid wedge yet |
| Cheap Pro | **~$9/mo** | Thin paid layer only |
| Annual | **~$59–72/yr** | Lower friction for long search |
| Search pack | **$49–59 / ~90 days** | Episodic job hunt (2–4 mo) |

**Avoid now:** sole $19–29/mo, early lifetime.

**$19–30/mo only after wedge:** AI volume and/or real ATS/JD scans.

### Packaging sketch

| Tier | Price | Gate |
|------|-------|------|
| Free | $0 | Full core apply loop |
| Pro | ~$9/mo or pack | Extra AI quota; analytics if ready |
| Search | ~$19/mo or $49–59/90d | Match volume + ATS scans **when shipped** |

Code already has soft AI caps (5 rewrites / 2 summaries / 5 matches/mo when AI on)—metering path exists; billing does not.

---

## Decision gates (before billing code)

1. Pick packaging: **Pro+quotas** vs **scan-led Search**.  
2. Ship the paid wedge first (AI volume and/or JD-ATS).  
3. Re-scrape competitor list prices.  
4. Explicit product approval (PLAN).

---

## Bottom line

**Win today:** free unlimited build + export + track + fill.  
**Lose today:** JD-ATS depth, board clip, AI breadth.  
**Price today:** free or ~$9 / pack—not Teal/Rezi monthly until the paid reason is real.
