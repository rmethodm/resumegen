## Possible Upgrades & Enhancements

### AI & Content Intelligence
| Feature                       | What it does                                                                                                       | Who has it                     |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| **Job description tailoring** | Paste a job posting → AI rewrites/reorders your resume to match keywords and requirements, with a live match score | Teal, Rezi, ResuFit            |
| **Grammar & spell check**     | Real-time proofreading with suggestions inline in the editor                                                       | Enhancv, Resume.io             |
| **Resume strength score**     | Actionable checklist beyond ATS (completeness, quantified bullets, action verbs, length)                           | Kickresume, Novoresume         |
| **Interview prep**            | AI generates likely interview questions + sample answers from your resume + a target job description               | Enhancv, Teal Pro, SkillsFirst |
| **Career path suggestions**   | AI suggests logical next roles based on your current resume                                                        | SkillsFirst                    |

### Import & Integrations
| Feature              | What it does                                                                | Who has it                          |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **LinkedIn import**  | One-click: scrape your LinkedIn profile → pre-fill all resume fields        | Teal, Kickresume, Resume.io         |
| **DOCX export**      | Download resume as a Word document (many employers still require it)        | Zety, Resume.io, most major players |
<!-- | **Chrome extension** | Save job postings from any board with one click → populates the job tracker | Teal                                | -->

### Job Search Workflow
| Feature                 | What it does                                                                                             | Who has it                |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Resume versioning**   | Tag tailored copies of the same resume to specific jobs in the tracker; see which version you sent where | Teal, Rezi                |
| **Salary insights**     | Show market salary range for a target role/location when tailoring to a job                              | LinkedIn Premium, Teal    |
| **Follow-up reminders** | Set a date to follow up on a job application; email notification fires                                   | Teal, manual job trackers |

### Sharing & Visibility
| Feature                         | What it does                                                                                                                 | Who has it          |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| **QR code for share link**      | Generate a printable QR code that points to the public view                                                                  | Enhancv, Kickresume |
| **Video intro on public view**  | Short 30–60s video embedded on the share page alongside the resume                                                           | Enhancv             |
| **Social proof / view counter** | Show the resume owner "your resume has been viewed X times this week" (already have analytics, just surface it more visibly) | Teal, Enhancv       |

### Platform & Monetization
| Feature                        | What it does                                                            | Who has it                |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Recruiter/agency plan**      | Multi-resume management for recruiters; client login or read-only share | Kickresume Teams, Enhancv |
| **Referral program**           | Users get a free month / credits for each paying referral               | Common in SaaS            |
| **Multi-language UI + resume** | Build and export resume in a non-English language                       | Novoresume, Resume.io     |
| **White-label**                | Agencies can use Resumegen under their own brand                        | Niche, but high-value     |

### UX & Polish
| Feature                     | What it does                                                                                       | Who has it       |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| **Mobile-optimized editor** | Usable editing experience on iPhone (right now the split-pane editor isn't great on small screens) | Resume.io, Canva |
| **Resume freshness nudges** | "Your last job entry is 8 months old — want to update?" scheduled emails                           | Teal             |
| **Dark mode**               | Editor + dashboard in dark mode                                                                    | Most modern SaaS |
