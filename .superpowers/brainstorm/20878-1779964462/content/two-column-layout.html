Two-Column Sidebar Layout

How should the sidebar be structured? This is the most popular alternative to single-column resumes.

A — Colored sidebar, contact + skills left

Accent-colored left column (35%) holds photo placeholder, contact info, and skills. Experience and education on the right. Bold, modern feel.

B — Light sidebar right, experience left

Experience and summary dominate the left (main) column. Light gray right sidebar (32%) holds contact, skills with bar indicators, and education. Professional and ATS-friendly.

C — Full-width header banner + two columns below

Bold dark header spans full width with name and title. Two equal-ish columns below split experience/summary (left) from education/skills/contact (right). Dramatic and clean.
