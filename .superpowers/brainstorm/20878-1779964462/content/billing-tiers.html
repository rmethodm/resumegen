Billing Model — Free vs. Paid

Pick the tier structure that fits your vision. This shapes what gets gated across all 4 subsystems.

A

Freemium — Light limits

Free: 1 resume, 3 templates, basic ATS score, 1 cover letter template.
Pro (~$9/mo): Unlimited resumes, all templates + colors + fonts, full ATS scoring, all cover letter templates, job tracker, undo/redo.

Pros

  • Low barrier to sign up
  • Users experience the product before paying

Cons

  • Power users on free tier put load on server
B

Freemium — Hard paywall on premium features (Recommended)

Free: unlimited resumes, 2 templates (classic + minimal), no ATS, no cover letters, no job tracker.
Pro (~$12/mo): All templates + colors + fonts, ATS scoring, cover letter templates, job tracker, undo/redo.

Pros

  • Clear value proposition for Pro
  • Free tier is genuinely useful (resumes + sharing)

Cons

  • Users need to pay before trying premium features
C

Free trial → Paid only

14-day free trial with full access, then $12/mo to continue. No permanent free tier.

Pros

  • Everyone experiences full product
  • Higher conversion pressure

Cons

  • Harder acquisition — users hesitant to start a trial
  • No viral sharing from free users
D

Free + Credits (usage-based)

Free forever for core features. AI suggestions, ATS scoring, and cover letter generation cost credits. Buy credit packs or subscribe for unlimited.

Pros

  • Pay-per-use feels fair
  • Low commitment

Cons

  • Complex to build and explain
  • Lower predictable revenue
